## REQUIRED fixes

1. **High — bind work-item approval cards to the draft revision, not delivery time.** Backend `db/migrations/0092_phone_push.sql:72`, `lib/phone-push.ts:56`, `lib/notify-owner.ts:231` and `lib/owner-actions.ts:151`. Reproduced on the disposable QA DB: park the original $100 draft overnight; pull it; replace its staged knowledge draft with a $900 revision; request approval again and create the revised threaded card; drain the old outbox. The new $900 card becomes decided while the delayed $100 card becomes open. POST approve on the $100 card returns `Emailed client@example.test — Revised $900 quote`; the actual domain dispatcher records one accepted fake email whose subject/body are the new $900 draft. No real email was sent. The SQL's latest-created-message rule and TS's approval_status-only work-item resolver agree on the wrong generation. **Exact change:** persist an immutable approval generation/content binding when the draft is submitted and carry it through parked outbox payloads, messages and actions. Reconcile by that binding and atomically reject a stale binding before approving/declining or sending. A delayed old outbox may retain history, but must neither close the revised card nor authorize the new draft. Add a real staged-email regression for park -> pull -> revise -> resubmit -> old drain -> old approval refused, new approval sends once.

2. **High — make folded summaries durable and retain their channel snapshots.** Backend `lib/notify-owner.ts:571` and `:574`. Two independent real DB/provider-boundary reproductions: (a) six ordinary rows parked with channels=['app'], followed by settings App off/Telegram on, produce three App alerts and a Telegram-only summary; the three folded rows never get their App summary. (b) with App-only and APNs 503 for the summary, every original row already has sent_at; catch only unclaims/reschedules them without clearing sent_at. A later healthy drain sends zero summary retries. The summary's history row has no outbox job, so its failure is permanent. **Exact change:** create a stable durable summary job with its own claimed lease, saved channels, channel/device receipts and throttle accounting; group folded rows by compatible saved channels. Link constituents transactionally to that job and only mark their delivery complete when the summary is delivered or safely delegated to a durable job. Retry failed channels without duplicating successful ones. Respect remaining hourly room for the summary. Add channel-switch, provider-failure and crash-boundary tests for folding, including recovery after the first three direct sends.

3. **Medium — preserve the Telegram title and link in the APNs visible body.** Backend `lib/providers/apns.ts:22`. Generic `aps.alert.title='SJC OS'` is appropriate for the R1 privacy change, but copying only m.body loses the actual subject. The fresh payment payload with title `Payment received: $8,725 from Larson`, body=null and a money href yields `alert:{title:'SJC OS',body:''}`. The subject and URL survive in the feed/structured destination, not in the visible notification; title-only SMS opt-out, approval nudges and urgent-item call sites are similarly affected. Telegram still shows `[SJC OS] <title>\n<body>\n<url>`. **Exact change:** keep the generic APNs title and hidden-preview settings; compose a visible body from the original title, optional body and normalized URL, then apply the existing Unicode-safe byte limit. Avoid repeating the generic title for conversational rows. Add title-only, title+body, payment/contract, long-Unicode and link-preservation payload tests. P1's content parity must not be satisfied merely by opening the feed.

4. **Medium — include conversation-outbox persistence in the protected legacy Telegram fallback.** Backend `lib/owner-reply-delivery.ts:60` and `lib/owner-actions.ts:67`. The new legacyFallback catch covers history preparation but ends before enqueueOwnerConversation. Injecting a BEFORE INSERT push_outbox failure, then posting a real Telegram reply to a mapped decision, leaves the owner note delivered to its agent but produces zero Telegram transport calls. Webhook returns HTTP 200 with `{ok:false,error:'R2 outbox unavailable'}` after persisting the update, so Telegram will not redeliver it. The existing history-failure test passes because it fails inside the earlier catch. **Exact change:** protect all new pre-transport history/settings/outbox preparation on the established Telegram path. If durable enqueue fails before any transport attempt, use the permitted original Telegram transport and preserve its reply mapping; otherwise retain durable evidence and never fall back after an uncertain send. Keep the phone path fail-closed. Add actual-webhook tests for both history and outbox insert failure proving the note and acknowledgment survive without double-send.
