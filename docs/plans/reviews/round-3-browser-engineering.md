## Wrong or invented

- **Vault-off currently conflicts with manual privacy.** §13 lines 3026–3032 says effective flags are checked at admission/every action and “Vault flag off … closes private contexts.” §§5.2/12 expressly promise Phase 1 private manual take-over with vault disabled. The runtime privacy enum cannot distinguish these cases by itself: both are `private`. Following the flag rule literally closes a Phase 1 context immediately after take-over; ignoring it leaves vault revocation behavior underspecified. The same flag paragraph enumerates submit/pay/sign/upload cancellation but omits the new company lookup/save-page kinds, despite their owner-release requirement.
- **The response-side buffer envelope is missing.** §3.2 lines 279–295 disables automatic large-body streaming and describes reservations during **request** body collection, but specifies no response reservation/streaming policy. Mitmproxy buffers responses by default; `body_size_limit` is per message, not an aggregate heap budget. At §3.3's two contexts × eight active upstreams, roughly sixteen 25 MB responses can coexist, plus request buffers, parser/TLS/interpreter state and copies, inside a 384 MiB gate process. The new 56 MiB request-body limit does not establish a response limit. Ordinary authorized GET/resource traffic can therefore OOM the shared gate and trigger the root-reset latch; per-file quarantine limits and the aggregate slice cap do not prevent that. See official [buffering/streaming behavior](https://docs.mitmproxy.org/stable/overview/features/#streaming) and [body-size/stream options](https://docs.mitmproxy.org/stable/concepts/options/).
